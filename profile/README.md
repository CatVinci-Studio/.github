### Who we are

AI for everyone, not just the few. We build free tools that bring AI into the everyday work of ordinary people — writing, reading, organising — with nothing new to learn and nothing to pay for.

### Philosophy

- **Free and open source** — every line of code is public; anyone can use, check and improve it, free of charge.
- **No barriers** — nothing new to learn and no technical know-how needed: open it and start.
- **Made for everyone** — for students, writers and anyone who works with words, in ten languages and counting.
- **Privacy first** — your files stay on your own computer, and you decide which AI to use and what it sees.
