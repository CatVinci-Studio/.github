### Who we are · 我们是谁

AI for everyone, not just the few. We build free tools that bring AI into the everyday work of ordinary people — writing, reading, organising — with nothing new to learn and nothing to pay for.

让 AI 属于每一个人，而不只是少数人。我们做免费的工具，把 AI 带进普通人每天的写作、阅读和整理之中 —— 不用重新学，也不用付费。

### Philosophy · 理念

| | |
|---|---|
| **Free and open source** · 免费开源 | Every line of code is public; anyone can use, check and improve it. · 每一行代码都公开，任何人都可以免费使用、查看和改进。 |
| **No barriers** · 无门槛 | Nothing new to learn, no technical know-how needed. · 不用学新东西，也不用懂技术，打开就能用。 |
| **Made for everyone** · 为所有人制作 | For students, writers and anyone who works with words, in ten languages. · 为每一个与文字打交道的人而做，已支持十种语言。 |
| **Privacy first** · 隐私保护 | Your files stay on your computer; you decide which AI to use and what it sees. · 文件留在你自己的电脑里，用哪个 AI、让它看到什么，由你决定。 |
